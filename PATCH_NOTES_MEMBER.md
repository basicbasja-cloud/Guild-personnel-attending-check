# Patch Notes for Members — June 12, 2026

---

## 🏋️ Training System is Here!

We now have a full training management system alongside the main war system:

- **📅 Calendar Events** — When you open the calendar, you'll see different event types with their own icons: ⚔️ War, 🏋️ Training, 📅 Internal Events, etc.
- **📋 Training Attendance** — When a training event is created, you'll see it on the calendar. Click it to respond **Join ✅ / Can't Join ❌ / Maybe 🤔** — just like war attendance!
- **🔔 Notifications** — A floating toast will appear when new training events are scheduled. There's also an auto-announcement posted so you won't miss it.
